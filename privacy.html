Privacy Policy

YY Trading Internal Dashboard · Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Who we are

The YY Trading Internal Dashboard (the "Dashboard") is operated by YY Trading, a second-hand clothing shop in Thailand. It is an internal tool used only to view statistics of YY Trading's own TikTok account.

2. TikTok data we access

The Dashboard connects to TikTok only with the following permissions (scopes), and accesses nothing beyond them:

  • user.info.basic – account display name and profile picture;
  • user.info.stats – follower count, following count, total likes and video count;
  • video.list – the list of the account's recent public videos and their basic details.

We do not access private messages, passwords, or data of any other TikTok user, and the Dashboard does not post or change anything on TikTok.

3. How we use the data

The data is used solely to display account statistics inside our internal Dashboard. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. No selling or sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose TikTok data to any third party.

5. Storage and access

We store only what is necessary to show the statistics in the Dashboard, and only for as long as it is needed for that purpose. Access is limited to authorized YY Trading staff, and access tokens are kept securely and never made public.

6. Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time by either of these methods:

  • in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions and remove "YY Trading Internal Dashboard"; or
  • email us at waywaytherdding387@gmail.com and ask us to disconnect the account.

Once access is revoked, the Dashboard can no longer retrieve data from TikTok.

7. Requesting data deletion

To have stored TikTok data deleted, email waywaytherdding387@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete the data and any stored access tokens within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to waywaytherdding387@gmail.com.
